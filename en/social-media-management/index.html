I run your business’s social media with strategy and consistency, so you don’t have to think about what to post every day. You take care of your business; I make sure it gets seen.

Method

Step by step

Six steps repeated every month, always based on your goals and your audience. No generic templates.

  1. Analysis

    What you want to achieve, who you’re speaking to, how your social media is doing and what your competitors are doing.

  2. Strategy

    Based on your goals and your target audience: tone, topics and formats designed for your business.

  3. Calendar

    What gets posted and when, and which goal each post serves.

  4. Creation

    Design, video editing and copy in your brand’s look and for your audience.

  5. Posting

    I schedule and post when your audience is most active. You just approve.

  6. Measurement

    A monthly report against your goals: what worked and what we adjust.
