About me

I’m Patri. I always wear sneakers and comfort comes first, but I love looking cute and feminine. That’s why I talk about fashion from both sides: you don’t have to suffer to look cute.

I don’t stop at what’s in: I explain what it says, why it’s appearing now and how to wear it as yourself. Because for me, getting dressed is also building yourself.

How I talk about fashion

My filter

Five questions I ask myself about every trend.

  1. What’s in?

    The trend, straight up.

  2. What does it say?

    What it communicates when you wear it.

  3. Why now?

    Where it comes from and why it’s appearing.

  4. What identity does it build?

    The girl behind the mix.

  5. How do you wear it as yourself?

    A comfy, cute version, adapted to you.
