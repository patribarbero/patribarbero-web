Privacy

Last updated: 21 November 2026

Who processes your data

Controller: Victoria Patricia Barbero Moyá (“Patri Barbero”). Email: patribarberoinfo@gmail.com.

What data I collect and why

I only collect the data you send me through the contact form or by email: name, email, brand or business, and whatever you tell me in your message (for example, sector, city, deadlines or budget).

I use it only to reply to you and, if we work together, to prepare and manage the proposal or project. I don’t use it to send you advertising and I don’t sell it.

Legal basis

Your consent, which you give by ticking the form box or writing to me, and taking pre-contractual steps when you ask me for a proposal.

How long I keep it

For as long as needed to reply and manage our relationship. If we don’t end up working together, I delete it within one year at most. If there is a project, I keep it for the periods required by law.

Who else sees it

The form is sent to my email through FormSubmit (formsubmit.co), a third-party service that only delivers the message. I receive and keep messages in Gmail (Google). The website is hosted by Cloudflare. These providers may process data outside the European Economic Area with the safeguards required by law.

I don’t share your data with anyone else, unless required by law.

Cookies

This website doesn’t use its own cookies, analytics cookies or advertising cookies.

YouTube videos only load if you click on them, and they load from youtube-nocookie.com. At that point, YouTube may store information in your browser under its own policy.

Your rights

You can ask me to access, correct, delete, restrict, object to the use of or port your data by writing to patribarberoinfo@gmail.com. You can also withdraw your consent at any time.

If you think I haven’t handled your data properly, you can file a complaint with the Spanish Data Protection Agency (aepd.es).

Minors

If you are under 14, please don’t send me your data through the form.
