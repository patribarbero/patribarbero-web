Process

From brief to delivery

Before we start, we agree in writing on the deliverables, the revisions, and where and for how long you can use the content.

  1. Brief

    You tell me the product, the goal and where it will be used.

  2. Proposal

    Approach, deliverables, deadlines, usage rights and a fixed quote.

  3. Product

    You send me the product or access to the service.

  4. Ideas and script

    I send you the hooks and the script before filming.

  5. Filming and editing

    I film, edit, subtitle and adapt to each format.

  6. Delivery

    Final files, with at least one round of revisions.
